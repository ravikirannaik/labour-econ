# How to Use This Site

This site is a companion to your lectures and the Borjas textbook. It is not a replacement for either -- it is designed to reinforce what you learn in class, provide practice opportunities, and connect the theory to Indian labour markets.

---

## How the Site Is Organised

| Section | What It Contains |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Chapters** | 11 chapter pages with content summaries, practice problems, and resources |
| **Indian Context** | Essays connecting labour economics theory to India's labour market |
| **Stata** | Installation guide, basic commands, and course-specific exercises |
| **Glossary** | Quick-reference definitions of ~80 labour economics terms |
| **References** | Full bibliography of textbooks and empirical papers |

---

## Recommended Workflow (Per Chapter)

1. **Before class:** Read the assigned Borjas chapter (20-30 pages)
2. **After class:** Review the chapter **Content** page for key takeaways and Indian context
3. **Self-test:** Work through the **Practice** problems
4. **Go deeper:** Explore the **Resources** page for empirical papers and videos
5. **For assignments:** Use the **Stata** section and Indian Context pages

---

## Navigation Tips

- Use the **tabs** at the top to jump between major sections
- Use the **sidebar** to navigate within a section
- Press ++s++ to open **search** -- find any term or concept instantly
- Toggle **dark mode** using the sun/moon icon in the header
- The **table of contents** on the right tracks your position on long pages

---

## About the Practice Problems

Practice problems use collapsible answers -- click to reveal the solution. Questions progress from basic recall to analysis and evaluation.
